# Assessing the Financial Health and Risk of S&P 500 Companies through PCA and Clustering

Implementation of the final project. *"Assessing the Financial Health and Risk of S&P 500 Companies through PCA and Clustering: Beyond the Risk-Return Map"* (F1009 – Analysis of Mathematical Methods for Physics, Tecnológico de Monterrey, Campus Guadalajara). The full synthesis of the project is available in the [final PDF](Financial_health_pc_clustering.pdf).

## Overview

This project evaluates the financial health of S&P 500 companies by combining market-risk metrics with fundamental accounting indicators. Eleven financial and market-risk variables are collected for 487 companies over a five-year period, reduced via Principal Component Analysis (PCA), and clustered with K-Means. The results show that a two-dimensional risk-return map alone is insufficient to capture financial health.
